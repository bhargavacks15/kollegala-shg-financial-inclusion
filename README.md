# SHG–Bank Linkage and Women's Financial Inclusion in Kollegala

## Project Overview

This  economics research internship project examines how self-help groups (SHGs), locally known as *sanghas*, connect women to savings and credit in Kollegala taluk, Chamarajanagar district, Karnataka.

The study combines published research, official statistics and informal oral interviews with members of three SHGs in Linganapura and Kempanapalya.

## Research Objectives

- Examine savings and internal lending practices within SHGs.
- Understand the distinction between group-based credit and individual bank borrowing.
- Explore reported repayment challenges and group inactivity.
- Assess the available evidence on women's financial inclusion and decision-making.

## Methodology

The project uses secondary data from published research and official reports, alongside informal oral interviews with members of three SHGs. The interviews were exploratory and the findings are not representative of all SHGs in Kollegala.

Reported loan amounts and repayment arrangements were not independently verified against bank records. The project does not estimate causal effects.

## Key Areas of Analysis

- SHG–Bank Linkage Programme statistics
- Local savings and lending practices
- Borrowing sources and financial inclusion
- Repayment challenges and limitations of the available evidence
- Recommendations for improving financial inclusion

## Limitations

The interview sample is small and based on participant availability. The published local survey contains inconsistencies, and official SHG-level statistics for Kollegala were not available to the researcher.
